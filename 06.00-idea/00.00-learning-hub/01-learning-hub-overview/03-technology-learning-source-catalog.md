## 📚 What this catalog is

A source list, nothing more. Sources are **read where you decide they live** and in **any medium** - a feed,
a paper, a recorded session, a transcript. Adding one here does not commit you to reading it; the routine
decides what earns attention, and the Hub's job is to turn what you meet into knowledge that is yours.

Star ratings are a **relevance-to-me** signal, not a quality ranking.

## 🎥 Non-text sources

The catalog is deliberately not text-only - the `generalized-content-engine` principle covers **any medium**.
These sources are handled by the same pipeline: fetched, transcribed or extracted where needed, then
developed like any other piece.

| Name<br/>Link | Description | Status information |
|---------------|-------------|--------------------|
| [Microsoft Build session catalog](https://build.microsoft.com/) | Recorded conference sessions with transcripts - a flagship channel with its own ingestion path | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐<br/>Annual, deep<br/>Video + transcript |
| [Microsoft Ignite session catalog](https://ignite.microsoft.com/) | Recorded sessions, slides and proceedings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐<br/>Annual, deep<br/>Video + transcript |
| [Azure Friday](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/azure-friday/) | Short product-team demonstrations | ⭐⭐⭐<br/>Weekly<br/>Video |
| [.NET Conf](https://www.dotnetconf.net/) | Recorded sessions on the .NET platform | ⭐⭐⭐⭐<br/>Annual<br/>Video |
| [CNCF YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@cncf) | KubeCon and project talks | ⭐⭐⭐<br/>Continuous<br/>Video |
| Your own meetings and talks | Recordings, transcripts and notes you already hold - read in place, never copied into the public repository | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐<br/>Continuous<br/>Audio + transcript |

Diagrams, charts and architecture images are also first-class inputs; they are usually attached to one of the
sources above rather than being a channel of their own.
