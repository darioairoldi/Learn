## 🎯 Scope

This is Appendix C of [How Azure AI billing works](00.010-how-azure-ai-billing-works.md). It lists representative <mark>Azure AI Search list prices as of July 2026</mark> for Global/US deployments. Prices vary by region, currency, and agreement, and they change often — always confirm on the linked pricing pages.

Dedicated Azure AI Search bills by <mark>search units (SUs)</mark>, where `SU = replicas × partitions`. For how SUs, premium features, and Foundry IQ fit together, see [Azure AI Search billing in all its flavors](00.010-how-azure-ai-billing-works.md#-azure-ai-search-billing-in-all-its-flavors).

## 🔎 Search unit prices

Per SU / month, dedicated, default compute:

| Tier | Storage per partition | Price per SU/month |
|---|---|---|
| <mark>Free</mark> | 50 MB | $0 |
| <mark>Basic</mark> | 15 GB | $73.73 |
| <mark>S1</mark> | 160 GB | $245.28 |
| <mark>S2</mark> | 512 GB | $981.12 |
| <mark>S3</mark> | 1 TB | $1,962.24 |
| <mark>L1 (storage optimized)</mark> | 2 TB | $2,802.47 |
| <mark>L2 (storage optimized)</mark> | 4 TB | $5,604.21 |

Total monthly cost = price per SU × replicas × partitions. For example, an S1 service with 2 replicas and 2 partitions = 4 SUs × $245.28 ≈ **$981/month**. Confidential-compute variants cost more, and serverless (compute-unit) billing is expected to begin in late 2026.

## ➕ Premium features billed on top

| Feature | Free allowance | Rate beyond allowance |
|---|---|---|
| <mark>Semantic ranker</mark> | First 1,000 queries/month | <mark>**$1 per 1,000 queries**</mark> |
| <mark>Agentic retrieval</mark> | First 50M tokens/month | <mark>**$0.022 per 1M (min/low effort) to $0.10 per 1M (medium)**</mark> |
| <mark>Custom Entity Lookup skill</mark> | none | <mark>**$1 per 1,000 text records** (tiered down to $0.25)</mark> |
| <mark>Image extraction (document cracking)</mark> | none | <mark>**$1 per 1,000 images** (tiered down to $0.65)</mark> |

Agentic retrieval also incurs Azure OpenAI token charges for query planning and answer synthesis on your model deployment, billed at the token rates in [Appendix A](00.011-appendix-a-billing-by-tokens.md).
