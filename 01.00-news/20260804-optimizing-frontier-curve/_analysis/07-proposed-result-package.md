## ✅ Concise answer

The article signals a move from "frontier model" thinking to **frontier system** thinking. Specialized models and strong harnesses can handle common work efficiently, while routers reserve expensive general models for difficult cases. Evaluations define the target, production feedback moves the frontier, and model-neutral architecture preserves both bargaining choice and continuity.

For the Learning Hub, the impact is additive: consolidate scattered model-routing, token-efficiency, evaluation, and independence guidance into one canonical Analysis article. The published observation links only to that article and external sources; working analysis remains internal. No vision amendment or PE architecture change is needed.
